Privacy Policy

Glyph: Draw the Light · KZN Games · effective 31 October 2026

Glyph: Draw the Light ("Glyph", "the game") is a game for Android by KZN Games. This policy explains what the game does with information about you. The short version: it collects nothing.

What we collect

Nothing. Glyph has no accounts, no sign-in, no advertising, no analytics, no in-app purchases and no third-party services. It does not request internet access and works entirely offline. No personal data, device identifiers, location, contacts or usage data are collected, sent or shared — with us or with anyone else.

What stays on your device

The game keeps a small save file in its own private storage on your phone: your best scores and best chains, whether you have played your first run, how far your runs have reached, and your settings (sound, music, haptics and their strength, the sound's voice and its sync, and whether Zen is chosen). It never leaves your device. Uninstalling the game, or clearing its storage in Android's settings, deletes it.

Permissions

The game asks for one permission: vibration, for the haptic feedback when you draw. You can turn haptics off in the game's settings.

Children

Glyph collects no information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If a future version changes any of this — for example by adding an online feature — this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the date above will change.
